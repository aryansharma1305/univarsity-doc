# Academic terminology

- **Program (Course):** The academic course in which a student enrols. Course Management uses this name in the staff interface.
- **Curriculum Version:** A particular syllabus of a program, with its own academic structure and subjects.
- **Academic Period:** One numbered semester or year within a curriculum version.
- **Subject:** A reusable academic subject in the university catalogue.
- **Subject Assignment:** A subject's placement, credits, classification and assessment configuration within a curriculum version.
- **Registration:** A student's enrolment in a program and academic session; it may follow a curriculum version.
