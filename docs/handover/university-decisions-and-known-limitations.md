# University decisions and known limitations

- **Audience:** university management, Registrar, Controller of Examinations, legal/privacy contact,
  the project mentor.
- **Scope:** decisions the university still has to make (the system blocks or fails closed until
  then) and limitations of the released system.
- **Applies to release `916b07c`.**

Related lists: [docs/database/open-questions.md](../database/open-questions.md) (numbered client
questions), ADR-0014 to ADR-0018, ADR-0023, ADR-0024.

## Known limitations

- **Template editors are code-defined.** Certificate and marksheet templates are previews with
  invented data; there is no template editor in the Admin Panel. Official documents are PDFs the
  university supplies and uploads.
- **PDF metadata stripping is a follow-up.** Image uploads are re-encoded without metadata; PDFs
  (historical documents and student uploads) are stored and delivered as uploaded, including author,
  XMP metadata and embedded image EXIF (open question 21, ADR-0023).
- **Student password change** from Account Settings is not available; recovery is a new activation
  code.
- **Staff account creation and role changes** are CLI-only in this release (PR #61 pending).
- **Examination timetable** is not available (PR #62 pending). Docversity never runs, schedules or
  marks examinations.
- **Result withdrawal** has no re-publication or correction API.
- **Revoked documents** cannot be re-issued; prepare a new document.
- **Marksheets published before migration `20261129090000_result_verification_token`** have no QR
  credential (status-only verification).
- **Certificate applications** stay unavailable until an approved certificate policy with application
  rules exists for the course; Finance, Rector approval and payment for certificates are not built.
- **Redis is not in backups**; queue recovery beyond the readiness refusal gate is not designed for
  real-data operation (Hostinger runbook).
- **Legacy WordPress records and legacy QR links** are not migrated.
- **Dashboard workload counts and reports** are limited in this release (PR #60 pending).
