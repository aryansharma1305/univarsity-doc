# Docversity web product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students frequently access their own university records and services from phones. Staff manage
academic records through a separate authenticated admin workspace.

## Product Purpose

A university academic verification and records portal. Student success means recognizing their
identity and course, finding documents and examination access, and understanding request status.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing student routes provide profile/correction requests, assigned curricula, examination app
links, re-exam applications and regional payment instructions, published documents and account
information. APIs enforce student ownership. UI-03 changes presentation only; authentication,
permissions, fees, attempts, academic rules, migrations and stored records remain unchanged.
The Results page provides authenticated empty/error states while publication remains unavailable;
the notifications page remains honestly unavailable. No unpublished marks, fabricated
progress, notifications or university branding. Payment verification and application decisions
remain separate.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Docversity's established UI-01/UI-02 identity and installed GSAP/Motion patterns. The student
experience is warmer and calmer, with mobile quality equal to desktop quality.

## Product Principles

- Prioritize frequent student tasks at 390px.
- Use only authorized real API data, with honest unavailable states.
- Keep reading and navigation immediate; animation is optional enhancement.
- Preserve all existing academic and payment behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA targets, keyboard access, practical touch targets, accessible drawer focus management
and equivalent reduced-motion behavior.
